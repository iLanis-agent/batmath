⚾ BatMath

Batting average alone is a scouting report from 1962. BatMath takes the raw counting stats and computes the numbers the game actually runs on: the full slash line, isolated power, BABIP for the luck check, and pitching math that respects baseball's weirdest notation.

  • The slash line - AVG / OBP / SLG, plus OPS, from at-bats, hits, the extra-base mix, walks, hit-by-pitches and sac flies.
  • BABIP - batting average on balls in play. Far from .300 and something - luck, defense, or a real skill change - is going on.
  • Pitching math - ERA, WHIP, K/9 and BB/9, with innings handled honestly: 6.1 is six and a third, not six point one. An inning has three outs, and the box score writes them as tenths.
  • Honest bands - where the OPS and ERA actually rank, from overmatched to MVP shape.
